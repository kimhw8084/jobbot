# Career strategy

The durable career architecture is separate from live retrieval. The long-term allocation remains **55% Healthcare Regulated Operations & Data Quality, 25% Healthcare Analytics/Project/Implementation, 15% Education/Learning/EdTech, and 5% Bilingual AI/Content Quality**. The lane percentages in `config/strategy.toml` add to those four groups; they do not set search-query counts or limit which enabled family is retrieved.

| Career group | Allocation | Included lanes |
|---|---:|---|
| Healthcare Regulated Operations & Data Quality | 55% | Healthcare operations/access 35%; healthcare quality/data/analytics 20% |
| Healthcare Analytics/Project/Implementation | 25% | Health information/documentation/QA 15%; healthcare implementation/project/program 10% |
| Education/Learning/EdTech | 15% | Higher education/EdTech operations 15% |
| Bilingual AI/Content Quality | 5% | Content/bilingual/AI quality 5% |

The active live-search configuration is separately versioned in `config/live_search.toml`. The Sept. 22, 2026 initial ordering is a calibration seed: provider lifecycle/credentialing/provider data; nonclinical healthcare quality/data/documentation/compliance; realistic healthcare project/implementation/program support; higher-ed academic back-office; bounded responsibility-matched transferable records/data/process operations; back-office patient access/eligibility/enrollment; permanent full-time bilingual AI/content quality. Fast runs prioritize the first three families. Deep and staged runs include a recall pass for every enabled credible family on LinkedIn, Indeed, and Glassdoor. The order can be recalibrated from CHG-114's durable yield/cost evidence; it is not a permanent quota or a retrieval gate.

Higher-ed academic back-office roles, established organizations, and low-interruption task/deadline work are ranking preferences. They do not remove discoveries. Phone/chat queues, continuous live intake, admissions sales, and customer-success work rank lower unless a separate hard gate fails. Transferable retrieval is limited to the configured records, data-quality, validation, and process-operation family; generic corporate operations is not an open-ended fallback.

Search recall is broad; final recommendations remain fail-closed in the existing CHG-113-compatible qualification path. An actionable recommendation requires positively stored evidence for home-only U.S. remote eligibility compatible with Texas, full-time permanent employment, verified employer-provided USD base pay of at least $24/hour or $49,920/year, zero mandatory travel and in-person requirements, a current/open posting, supported mandatory requirements, substantive domain/responsibility evidence, and a new/unhandled ledger status. Unknown facts stay in review. Title-only words such as registrar, admissions, enrollment, intake, operations, coordinator, and implementation do not establish role fit by themselves. No-repeat surfacing preserves the discovery and application history while preventing handled items from returning as new actionable opportunities.

Candidate evidence remains bounded by the configured record: healthcare/patient enrollment and documentation experience does not establish direct provider enrollment or credentialing ownership. Training is not professional ownership. See [Search plan](SEARCH_PLAN.md) for exact pass structure and plan fields.
